I am a PhD student in Computer Science at King's College London, advised by Prof. Grigorios Loukides. My research interests span algorithms and data mining (particularly at scale), as well as machine learning topics including graph neural networks, LLM agents, and adversarial ML.

I am open to opportunities for collaboration and academic exchange in any form. Please feel free to contact me if you are interested.
